01 / Our Development Model

Five Areas.
One Complete Player.

Our training goes beyond isolated skills. We build the physical, technical, tactical and mental qualities required to perform when the pressure is real.

01

Batting

Balance, timing, shot selection, footwork and game-specific batting decisions.

02

Bowling

Run-up rhythm, release point, accuracy, variations and tactical bowling plans.

03

Fielding

Reaction speed, catching technique, throwing accuracy, movement and anticipation.

Every Session
Has A Reason.

Players should know what they are working on, why it matters and how they can improve. Our coaching model keeps every stage connected.

Player Assessment

Identify strengths, technical gaps, fitness level and current game awareness.

02

Focused Training

Build specific skills through progressive drills and purposeful repetition.

03

Match Scenarios

Transfer training into realistic game situations and competitive decision-making.

04

Coach Feedback

Review performance and set clear next-step goals for the next training cycle.
