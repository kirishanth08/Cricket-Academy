01 / Our Story

Started With A Simple Idea.

Great cricket development needs more than access to a ground. It needs knowledgeable coaches, purposeful sessions, consistent feedback and an environment where players are encouraged to challenge themselves.

Elite Cricket Academy was built around that idea. We bring structured coaching and competitive experience together to help players understand not only how to play, but why the game demands certain decisions.

From a beginner learning their first stance to an ambitious player preparing for selection trials, our goal remains the same: create better players and better people through cricket.

Rahul SharmaFounder & Head Coach
