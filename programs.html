02 / What Training Covers

More Than
One Skill.

Every program combines the core disciplines of cricket with physical preparation and game intelligence. Players learn to connect what they practise with what they need to do in a match.

Batting

Balance, timing, footwork and shot selection.

Bowling

Accuracy, rhythm, variations and tactics.

Fielding

Reaction, catching, throwing and movement.

Fitness

Strength, speed, agility and conditioning.
